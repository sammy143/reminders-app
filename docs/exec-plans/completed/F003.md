# F003: buildSeries: escalation schedule with intensity and in-person rules

## Goal
Pure `buildSeries` in `src/domain/` turning an appointment + injected `now` into the
notification steps from docs/PLAN.md "Escalation schedule" (harness/feature_list.json F003).

## Approach
- `src/domain/series.ts`: `buildSeries(appointment, now: Date): SeriesStep[]`, using
  `computeLeaveBy` (F002). The `SeriesStep` type goes in `src/types/`: `{ step: 1..6, at: Date, tone: LadderTone, minutesFromLeaveBy: number }`.
- No `text` yet: lines come from the insult bank in F004, which will add `text` to each step
  (PLAN's `[{at, tone, text}]` is the end state after F004).
- Tests next to it: `series.test.ts`.

## Decisions
- Offsets from leaveBy: −30, −10, 0, +3, +6, +10 min (PLAN table), as one constant array (2026-09-24).
- Base tones per step: polite, firm, sarcastic, rude, savage, unhinged.
- Intensity caps the tone on the ladder polite < firm < sarcastic < rude < savage < unhinged:
  mild → at most sarcastic; spicy → at most savage; savage → uncapped. PLAN only says "mild
  tops out at firm/sarcastic"; capping (rather than shifting every step down) keeps steps 1–3
  identical across intensities, and later steps stay at least as harsh as earlier ones (2026-09-24).
- Not in-person → only step 1 (polite), whatever the intensity.
- Past steps: drop steps with `at < now` (strict), and keep the original step numbers so F004
  can pick lines per step. A step exactly at `now` is kept (2026-09-24).
- Supportive tone ("I'm genuinely stuck") and snooze are out of scope; later features handle them.
- Not in-person (PLAN rule 2: one polite reminder, always): if step 1 is already past but
  `now < startsAt`, return step 1 pulled forward to `at = now`; from `startsAt` on (inclusive),
  return []. In-person keeps dropping past steps (all past → []) (2026-09-24, fix round 1).
- `SeriesStep.minutesFromLeaveBy` is the scheduled offset (−30, −10, 0, 3, 6, 10) for F004's
  practical cue. The pulled-forward non-in-person step keeps −30 (its scheduled slot); F004 should
  derive the "leave in N min" cue from `at` vs leaveBy if it needs the live value (2026-09-24).
- `LadderTone = Exclude<Tone, 'supportive'>` types the schedule, ladder, cap and `SeriesStep.tone`.
- `SeriesStep` is derived and never persisted; rebuild it from appointment + now.

### Notes for later features
- F004 keys lines by (step, tone, intensity) and needs at least 4 unique mild×sarcastic lines;
  mild×rude/savage/unhinged and spicy×unhinged can't happen.
- Line choice must be deterministic per (appointment.id, step), not by array index.
- F006 must clamp a step whose `at` is at or before now to "fire now".
- Snooze and supportive mode need a post-transform in F007.

## Verify
- Unit tests: in-person event yields 6 steps at -30,-10,0,+3,+6,+10 min from leaveBy
- Unit tests: non-in-person yields 1 polite step
- Unit tests: mild never exceeds sarcastic tone; steps already in the past are skipped (now injected)
- bash scripts/check.sh passes
- Extra: spicy tops out at savage; all past → []; step exactly at now kept; non-in-person
  pulled forward to now before startsAt, [] after; DST and midnight crossings keep absolute instants.
