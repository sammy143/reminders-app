# F007: "I've left" and "I'm genuinely stuck" actions (notification + in-app)

## Goal
A user in an active series can say "I've left" (the series stops, and the appointment is marked `left`) or "I'm
genuinely stuck" (the remaining series switches to supportive tone). Both work from the notification and in the app
(harness/feature_list.json F007; PLAN product rules 1 and 5; design `active-alarm.png`).

## Approach
As built after fix round 2; the reasons are in Decisions.
- **domain/**
  - `appointment.ts`: `markLeft` (any planned status → `left`) and `markStuck` (`scheduled`/`snoozed` →
    `stuck`) are pure and need `hasAlarm(appt)` (= in person, the one rule for Home, the alarm screen,
    planning and responses); anything else returns the same object (no save). `isPlanned` (`scheduled | snoozed |
    stuck` get notifications) and `isListed` (Home: planned + `left`) replace `isActive`. `applyEdit(appt, fields, now)`
    resets `left`/`stuck` to `scheduled` only on a real reschedule (`isReschedule`: `startsAt` moves and the
    new step 1 is still ahead); `timingChanged` lives here too.
  - `lines/select.ts`: `toSupportive(series)` keeps `step`, `at` and `minutesFromLeaveBy` and sets the tone to
    `supportive`; the generic `withLines` picks supportive lines with `k = step − 1` (unique: 6 lines per
    cell, F004 note). The cue stays the normal leave cue.
  - `notificationPlan.ts`: a `stuck` appointment plans its remaining steps (same `series:<id>:<step>` keys and
    times, at most 2 via `supportiveSeries`, stuck.ts) with supportive text, the neutral "starts at h:mm" cue
    and the `nagSupportive` category, never re-firing a due step; it still counts
    toward `IN_PERSON_SERIES_LIMIT`. The ladder of an in-person event has `nagSeries`; other reminders have no
    category. The diff compares key, time, body and category.
  - `notificationCategories.ts` (ids, buttons, titles) and `notificationActions.ts`
    (`toAppointmentAction`: series id + `left | stuck | open`, else null).
  - `alarm.ts`: `alarmUnderway(appt, now)` and `alarmView(appt, now)`, both built on `hasAlarm`.
  - `today.ts`: `groupUpcoming` lists `isListed`; `remaining` counts planned ones not started;
    `cardStatus.mark` (`left`/`stuck`); `nextNagLine` is supportive when stuck.
- **services/notifications.ts** (deep imports only; the trace stays green): `setup()` registers `nagSeries`
  (`left`, `stuck`) and `nagSupportive` (`left`), every button `opensAppToForeground`; `onResponse`
  (clears iOS's last response as it forwards one), `takeLastResponse` (`getLastNotificationResponse` +
  clear, cold start), `dismiss`, `listPresented`. A plain tap is `actionId: 'open'`.
- **state/**: store `leave(id)` (then clears that appointment's delivered nags from the tray) and
  `stuck(id)`: save, then apply, then sync. `keepImminentFor` excludes `left` and `stuck`.
  `useNotificationResponses(onOpen)` in `_layout.tsx` (handled response ids at module scope):
  `left` → `leave`, `stuck` → `stuck`, then open `/alarm/<id>`; for an event without an alarm the buttons
  are ignored and a tap opens the editor. Navigation errors are caught.
- **ui/**: `AlarmScreen` (active-alarm.png): full tone background, per-tone contrast text (`alarmTone.ts`),
  Back control, countdown, Nag, the current line, step dots, a white "I've left" button and a pill "I'm
  genuinely stuck" button (hidden when stuck); left/over show a calm confirmation with Back. Home cards
  whose series is under way open it; a left card shows "Left ✓" (`tone-done`), a stuck one "Stuck".
- **app/**: `alarm/[id].tsx`.

## Decisions
- **"I've left" in v1 just cancels** (PLAN rule 1: "Until v3, the button cancels the series"). There's no 10-min
  snooze or roast yet; those are v3, with the geofence. Snooze is out of scope (2026-09-25).
- **Actions open the app** (`opensAppToForeground: true` for both). In Expo Go, a background JS handler for
  action taps isn't reliable when the app has been killed. Opening the app guarantees the handler runs, and the
  alarm screen then confirms the result. Logged as possible polish for dev builds (2026-09-25).
- **Stuck can't be undone in v1** except by moving the start time (a reschedule resets the status; fix round 1). "Mute today" is F008 (2026-09-25).
- **Supportive lines** come from the F004 supportive cells at the appointment's intensity, with `k = step − 1` (2026-09-25).
- **Home lifecycle**: `isListed` is the Home filter (planned + `left`); `isPlanned` decides notifications
  (`scheduled`, `snoozed`, `stuck`); `isActive` is removed (2026-09-25, fix rounds 1–2).
- Unknown or foreign responses (an unknown appointment id, or an id not in the `series` namespace) are ignored safely.
- **Implementation choices and deviations** (2026-09-25, implementer):
  - **Category ids are `nagSeries` / `nagSupportive`**, not `nag-series` / `nag-supportive`: the installed
    `setNotificationCategoryAsync.d.ts` says "Don't use the characters `:` or `-` in your category identifier".
    Definitions (ids, buttons, titles) live in `domain/notificationCategories.ts`; the adapter only translates.
  - **Category is part of the diff**, read back from our own `data.category` (not `content.categoryIdentifier`,
    which Expo Go may scope). Notifications scheduled before F007 have none, so they are rescheduled once and
    gain their buttons. Reminders for events that aren't in person get no category (no buttons).
  - **Lifecycle helpers live in `domain/appointment.ts`**: `isPlanned` (notifications: scheduled, snoozed, stuck)
    and `isListed` (Home: planned + left). `isActive` is gone. `timingChanged` moved there from notificationPlan
    (applyEdit needs it). The scheduler's `keepImminentFor` uses `isPlanned`, so a left appointment's imminent
    nag is cancelled too.
  - **`markStuck` only from `scheduled`/`snoozed`**; `markLeft` from any planned status (stuck → left works).
    A no-op transition saves nothing (the store's `mutate` accepts a `null` change).
  - **`withLines` is generic** over ladder and supportive steps (`SupportiveStep` in types); supportive picks
    `k = step − 1`. A stuck appointment never re-plans its due step, even when just saved.
  - **Responses**: the adapter uses `getLastNotificationResponse` + `clearLastNotificationResponse` (the `…Async`
    variants are deprecated in 57.0.21) and maps `DEFAULT_ACTION_IDENTIFIER` to `open`. Each response has a
    `responseId` (identifier|date|action) so a cold-start response that also reaches the listener is handled
    once (module scope since fix round 1). After `left`/`stuck` the tapped notification is dismissed (`dismissNotificationAsync`; Android keeps
    it in the tray after a button tap). All three actions then open `/alarm/<id>`; a failed save still opens it
    (in-app buttons are the fallback).
  - **Category registration failures don't block scheduling**: logged once per attempt, retried on the next
    `setup()`; nags still go out without buttons.
  - **Alarm screen only for in-person** appointments (`hasAlarm`): "I've left" means nothing for a call.
  - **Alarm line** is the next `buildSeries` step (the pulled current step before the start) with a live cue.
  - **Design deviations** (active-alarm.png vs. the plan text; see DESIGN.md "Alarm screen"): "I've left" is a
    white (`bg-surface`) button with ink text, as in the mockup, not an ink button. The stuck button and all
    text use the per-tone contrast colour, not `tone-supportive` (fix round 1). The countdown uses the
    `countdown` token without `font-heading` (fonts aren't loaded yet; it fell back to serif on web). No
    material icons (none in the app). Nag is the existing mascot, no new variant.
  - **Fix round 1** (2026-09-25, reviewer/verifier):
    - `hasAlarm` shared by `alarmUnderway`, `alarmView` (online → `over`) and responses (online buttons
      ignored; a tap opens the editor).
    - **A rescheduled event starts a fresh series:** only moving `startsAt` resets `left`/`stuck` to
      `scheduled` (travel/buffer tweaks keep them), and that save passes no `savedId`, so past steps are
      dropped and no due step re-fires. Fix round 2 narrows it: `isReschedule(before, after, now)` also
      requires the new series' step 1 (new leaveBy − 30 min) to be after `now`, so fixing a slightly-off
      start after leaving keeps `left`/`stuck` and never resumes the ladder; `now` comes from the store's clock.
    - Per-tone contrast colours with a WCAG unit test from tailwind.config.js; supportive gets a 10% ink scrim
      (white on plain indigo is 4.47:1); no low-opacity text; the stuck button is a 44 px pill. DESIGN.md
      documents the alarm exceptions. A small "‹ Back" control on the active alarm.
    - The response listener clears iOS's last response; handled ids live at module scope (no re-apply on
      remount). `onOpen` errors are caught and logged.
    - `leave` clears the appointment's delivered nags (`getPresentedNotificationsAsync` +
      `dismissNotificationAsync`, deep imports traced green).
    - A stuck appointment's imminent ladder nag is cancelled (not in `keepImminentFor`).
    - No added quote marks around Nag's line (alarm and Home bubbles): bank lines may start with a quote.
    - Home's "N remaining" counts only planned appointments that haven't started.
    - Tech-debt: Expo Go category scoping unverified, stuck undo only by reschedule, buttons open the app.
  - **Fix round 2** (2026-09-25, reviewer):
    - `isReschedule` needs `now` (above).
    - `hasAlarm` moved to `domain/appointment.ts` (alarm.ts imports appointment.ts, so it can't live there
      without a cycle) and is used everywhere the rule applies: `markLeft`/`markStuck` return an event without
      an alarm unchanged (the store inherits it), and `planNotifications` uses it for the series quota and the
      category.
    - Not added: an architecture rule banning `.inPerson` reads in state/domain. A regex can't tell the rule
      from legitimate reads (the cue choice in `today.ts`/`lines/`, `limits.inPerson`, the form draft,
      `timingChanged`), so the allowlist would cover most files and prove little.
    - Dedupe test: a new action on the same notification, and the same key delivered again later, still get
      through.
  - **Home**: a left card shows "Left ✓" in `tone-done` and isn't dimmed; a stuck card shows "Stuck" in the
    supportive tone and keeps its countdown. Both replace the In person/Online badge text.

- **Stuck is shorter and softer** (2026-09-25, user request after PR #7):
  - At most 2 supportive nags: `supportiveSeries(appt)` (domain/stuck.ts) = the first 2 steps scheduled
    after `stuckAt`; later steps are cancelled and a step due at that moment never re-fires. If fewer remain,
    those are sent.
  - `stuckAt` (new optional `Appointment` field, ISO, validated by the storage schema; no migration) is set
    by `markStuck(appt, now)` and dropped by a reschedule. It fixes the pair, so a later sync (foreground
    top-up) never adds a third nag. A stuck appointment stored without it counts from step 1.
  - Supportive lines carry the neutral cue `formatStartsAtCue(startsAt)` ("starts at 3:00", local h:mm from
    the Date, same as `formatShortTime`), never a countdown or lateness. One template changed to read well
    with it ("When it clears, here's the time:" → "When it clears, remember:"). bank.test checks the
    supportive cells against the neutral cue and bans leave/late/now/min words in supportive text.
  - Alarm (stuck): the bubble shows the supportive line of the next supportive nag (the last once both
    fired) with the neutral cue; the countdown stays. `AlarmView.step` became `progress {current, total}`:
    "Step 5 of 6 · Escalation: Savage" on the ladder, "Supportive · 1 of 2" when stuck, "Supportive" with
    no dots when stuck came after the last step. Home's Nag line for a stuck appointment is its next
    supportive nag (none after the second).
  - Home cards keep the live countdown ("7 min late") in the supportive colour; the neutral cue applies to
    Nag's lines (notifications and bubbles), not the card's time readout.
